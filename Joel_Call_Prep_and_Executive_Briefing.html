6. The NDIS & Adaptive Athletics Strategy

Small Goods Gym is an Unregistered NDIS Provider delivering Evidence-Based Capacity Building for Plan-Managed and Self-Managed participants.

The Real NDIS Challenge in Australia
Plan Managers and Support Coordinators must justify continuing funding to the NDIA with objective functional outcomes. Passive clinic massages don't prove capacity building. Small Goods builds real strength and fall prevention.

How the Software Becomes an NDIS Superpower

  • 1-Click Audit-Ready Reports: Automatically exports isometric peak force from the Activforce 2 Dynamometer and power output from the Enode Accelerometer into a plan-review PDF.
  • Carer / Support Coordinator Portal: Secure read-only access for coordinators to download progress metrics without manual coach paperwork.
  • Adaptive Movement Presets: Built-in accommodations for seated/wheelchair lifting, grip-assist accessories, and unilateral variations.
